A classic Blackjack card game built with C# and Windows Forms. Play against the dealer, choose Hit or Stand, and try to get as close to 21 as possible without going over.

<img width="1123" height="687" alt="Blackjack screenshot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7eb3c5c4-b624-4810-b954-66fa6bbd6fc3" />

## 🎮 Features
- Play against a computer dealer
- **Hit** to draw another card, **Stand** to keep your hand
- Live totals for your hand and the dealer's hand
- Scoreboard that tracks wins, losses and draws

## 📜 Game Rules
- Number cards are worth their face value
- Jack, Queen and King are worth 10
- Aces are worth 1 or 11
- The dealer must keep drawing until reaching 17 or more
- Going over 21 is a bust and you lose the round
- If you and the dealer have the same total, it's a draw

## 💻 Requirements
- Windows
- [.NET 8] (check under Project → Properties → Target framework)
- Visual Studio 2022 or newer
